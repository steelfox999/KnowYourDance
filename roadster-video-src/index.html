Trick 1
Driving upside down
Up a ramp, onto a vertical wall, then along the underside of a ledge.
Fans suck air out from under the sealed floor.
Less pressure underneath, so the outside air pushes the car onto the surface.
Push from the air > weight, so it stays on the ceiling.
F = ΔP × A
pressure drop × floor area
Trick 2
Fall, roll, land softly
It launches off the ledge and rolls upright in mid-air. Cold-gas thrusters fire downward: compressed air rushes out, and the car is pushed the other way (Newton's 3rd law), so the fall slows to a soft touchdown.
Trick 3
Hopping over traffic
The underside thrusters fire together for a burst of lift. The car flies over the truck, twists, and lands.
The air supply runs out fast, so these are short hops, not sustained flight.
